# Privacy Policy — Digital Siddur

**Effective date:** July 21, 2026

Digital Siddur ("the app", "we") is a free, offline-first prayer book (siddur)
for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short
version: the app has no accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or
third-party tracking. Almost everything stays on your device.

## Data stored on your device

The following are saved **only on your device** (in the app's local storage)
and are never transmitted to us or anyone else:

- Your display and reading preferences (Hebrew / English display mode, theme,
  font size, comfort level).
- Your highlights and personal notes on prayers.
- Your reading position and last-used service.
- Your chosen location for prayer times (see below).

We cannot see this data, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Location

The app can calculate accurate halachic prayer times (zmanim) for where you
are. This is **optional**:

- If you grant location permission, the app reads your **approximate**
  ("when in use") location and sends the coordinates to **Hebcal**
  (https://www.hebcal.com), a third-party service, to retrieve the prayer
  times for your area. Your device may also convert the coordinates to a
  nearby city name.
- If you prefer not to share location, you can **search for your city by name
  instead**. That search text is sent to Hebcal to look up the city.

In both cases, the information is used **only** to compute prayer times for the
current request. We do not store your location on any server, do not link it to
your identity (there is no account), and never use it for advertising or
tracking. Hebcal's handling of these requests is governed by its own privacy
policy: https://www.hebcal.com/home/about/privacy-policy

Data sent to Hebcal is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** require an account or ask you to sign in.
- We do **not** show ads.
- We do **not** use analytics, crash reporting, advertising IDs, or any
  third-party tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** sell or share your personal information for advertising.
- We do **not** collect your contacts, photos, messages, or files.

## Children

The app is intended for users **age 13 and older**. It is not directed to
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
children under 13.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your content and settings are stored only on your device, you can
delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the
app. There is no server-side data for us to delete.
