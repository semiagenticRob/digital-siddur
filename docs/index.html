Digital Siddur

A free, offline-first weekday siddur — Nusach Ashkenaz, the Feigenbaum Weekday Siddur (Cohen Family Edition). Built for teens.

Read in Hebrew, English, or side-by-side, with inline commentary and insights. Highlight lines, keep private notes, and get accurate halachic times (zmanim) for your location. Completely free — no ads, no account, no tracking. Works offline.

Included

  • Weekday Shacharis, Minchah, and Maariv (including Motzaei Shabbos)
  • Mussaf for Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed, and Hallel
  • Birkas Hamazon, Al HaMichyah, and Borei Nefashos
  • Krias Shema al HaMitah, Sefiras HaOmer, Tefillas Haderech, Netilas Lulav
  • A glossary and the book’s front and back matter
